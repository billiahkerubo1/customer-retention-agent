# Customer Retention Strategies

**Document ID:** RET-001  
**Document type:** Demonstration retention policy  
**Status:** Illustrative sample, not an actual telecom company policy  
**Version:** 1.0

## Strategy 1: Early-Tenure Onboarding Support

**Objective:** Identify and resolve customer difficulties during the early stages of service adoption.

**Target customers:**
- Customers with tenure of 0 to 3 months.
- Customers reporting difficulties understanding or using their services.

**Recommended actions:**
- Contact the customer to understand their initial experience.
- Check whether the customer has experienced activation or service issues.
- Provide guidance on available services and support channels.
- Escalate unresolved technical issues to the appropriate support team.

**Restrictions:**
- Do not promise discounts or compensation.
- Do not assume that the customer is dissatisfied without confirming their experience.

**Approval:** Standard support actions must follow the organisation's customer-contact procedures.

## Strategy 2: Contract Review and Education

**Objective:** Help customers understand available contract options.

**Target customers:**
- Customers currently on month-to-month contracts.
- Customers who express interest in longer-term arrangements.

**Recommended actions:**
- Explain available contract options.
- Clarify the terms and conditions of each option.
- Establish whether the customer values flexibility or contract stability.

**Restrictions:**
- Do not automatically recommend a longer contract.
- Do not promise a lower price unless an approved offer explicitly permits it.

**Approval:** Contract changes must follow the organisation's contract approval procedures.

## Strategy 3: Technical Support Review

**Objective:** Identify and address service-related difficulties.

**Target customers:**
- Customers reporting connectivity or technical problems.
- Customers with unresolved technical support requests.

**Recommended actions:**
- Review available support history.
- Ask the customer whether the issue is ongoing.
- Refer unresolved issues to technical support.
- Confirm whether the issue has been resolved.

**Restrictions:**
- Do not claim that a technical issue exists based solely on a churn prediction.
- Do not claim that an issue has been resolved without confirmation.

**Approval:** Technical interventions must follow the organisation's support procedures.

## Important Policy Boundary

This document contains illustrative retention guidance for a learning project. It does not authorise discounts, credits, refunds, compensation, or specific commercial offers.

If a customer requests a financial incentive, the agent must retrieve an explicitly approved offer policy or report that no approved offer information is available.
